PROJECT: AI Resume Analyzer & Interview Preparation System
STAGE: Skill Gap Analysis

PURPOSE:
Compare candidate skills with job requirements and identify
matching skills and potential skill gaps.

OPTIMIZED PROMPT:

You are an experienced HR resume analyst.

Compare the candidate's skills mentioned in the resume with the
skills and requirements specified in the Software Developer
job description.

Identify:
1. Matching skills
2. Required skills not mentioned in the resume
3. Other relevant requirements and their availability

Do not assume or infer any information that is not explicitly
provided in the documents.

Do not conclude that the candidate lacks a skill simply because
it is not mentioned in the resume.

Present the analysis in a clear structured table.

OUTPUT FORMAT:

| Skill / Requirement | Job Requirement | Resume Information | Status |

Use "Not mentioned" when a required skill is not found in the
resume.
